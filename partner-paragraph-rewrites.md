### Option A — Authoritative / analytical
*Rationale: treats the stack as a deliberate architecture with one named gap, and lets our members own each layer. The standards appear only as the named slots they fill — and the slot they leave empty. Best for the law-review register.*

> A handful of open standards are paving the path to agentic commerce, and each is engineered for a single need: **x402** for payments, **ERC-8004** for agent identity and reputation, **A2A** for agent-to-agent negotiation. The substance, though, is in the ecosystem now building across every layer. On **payments**, x402 rails carry HTTP-native settlement, with OKX's unified agent-payment dispatch routing across x402, MPP and AP2, and MetaMask's ERC-7710/7715 delegated permissions granting agents bounded, revocable spending authority that never surrenders custody. The **identity and reputation** layer — the work ERC-8004 frames — is being built out by our members: 0G Labs (ERC-7857 intelligent NFTs for verifiable agent ownership), AltLayer (the 8004scan registry and explorer), BNB Chain (on-chain registration and storage), Starknet (ERC-8004 on a ZK-rollup), OpenServ (multi-agent identity), and Humanity Protocol and Humanode for proof-of-human. **Negotiation and interoperability** — the surface A2A describes — runs on Anoma's intent matching and OpenServ orchestration, while **escrow and obligations** are held by Arkhai's Alkahest conditional escrow and bounded by MetaMask delegation. **Execution and evidence** draw on 0G Labs, Heurist, io.net, Chutes and AntSeed for inference and compute, LI.FI for cross-chain movement, and Nansen, Chainbase and Collective Memory for analytics and time-stamped records. One layer is conspicuously absent from every standard: **verification and dispute.** Each leaves adjudication out by design. That is precisely where **Internet Court** plugs in — GenLayer's AI-validator jury for qualitative, natural-language judgment, with Intelligent Oracle for web-evidence factual questions and UMA's optimistic oracle as complementary verification tools, and connectors that turn a verdict into on-chain enforcement through ERC-7710 revocation.
