# Blog post writing guidelines

Read and follow these rules every time you create or modify a blog post in this directory. The reference for voice and format is `en/internet-court-agentic-commerce.mdx` (the main dispatch article).

## Punctuation

- Never use em dashes ("—", U+2014). No exceptions. Rewrite with commas, colons, parentheses, or two sentences.
- Avoid en dashes ("–") in prose; use them only inside numeric ranges.

## Names in bold

- Put company, partner, protocol, and product names in **bold** on every mention in prose: **GenLayer**, **MetaMask**, **AntSeed**, **Collective Memory**, **Kleros**, **x402**, and so on.
- Do not bold standard identifiers like ERC-7710, USDC, or HTTP, and do not bold "Internet Court" in normal prose.
- Never web-source partner logos; official ones live in the one-pager folder.

## Format

- Frontmatter: `title`, `date` (YYYY-MM-DD), `excerpt`, `tag` (usually "Dispatch").
- Headings are `###`, sentence case, no numbering.
- Pull quotes are one-line blockquotes (`> ...`): short, declarative, standalone. Two or three per article, placed between sections or after a key beat.
- End with short standalone lines as their own paragraphs for the closing beat (see how the main article ends).

## Voice

- Open on the broad shift or problem before naming the product.
- Short, punchy sentences. Concrete numbers over adjectives.
- Frame disputes as a clause agreed up front, not a venue you escalate to.
- Keep articles tight: cut scaffolding, keep concrete examples.

## Localization

- Write only the English version in `en/`. Translations (`es/`, `ko/`, `ru/`, `zh/`) are generated by the i18n build pipeline; never hand-edit or hand-create them.
