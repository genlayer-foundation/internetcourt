# Roadmap

Planned work for the internetcourt.org marketing site and agent experience.

## Marketing site

### Hero video treatments (in progress)
Explore 5 alternate hero (above-the-fold) layouts that each present the launch video differently — embedded/framed player, full-bleed background, split side-by-side, subtle backdrop, and cinematic band — viewable side by side on a preview route so we can pick a direction.

### Blog section on the homepage
Add a Blog section to the homepage to surface posts, announcements, and updates.

### "Chat to the Internet Court Agent" variant
For visitors who don't have an agent yet, offer a conversational variant that lets them chat with the Internet Court Agent directly — which can also walk them through installing the script.

## Internationalization

### Translations
Translate the entire site into:
- Korean

- Russian

## Discoverability

### SEO
Make the site SEO friendly — metadata, structured data, sitemaps, performance, and translated-page indexing.
